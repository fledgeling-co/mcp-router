## What was built

**Phase A — four appearance contexts.** `ColorToken` grows from 18 cases over two appearances to 40
over four resolved contexts. The values live in three files rather than one, because forty tokens
across four contexts does not fit under SwiftLint's 400-line file ceiling and its 250-line type-body
ceiling: `ColorToken.swift` declares the cases, `ColorToken+Appearance.swift` the two base
appearances, `ColorToken+IncreasedContrast.swift` the two `prefers-contrast` ones, and
`ColorToken+Role.swift` the role contract. Nine tokens re-solve for contrast; the other thirty-one
return their base from an explicit `default:` arm. Both spellings changed to the mock's —
`--lineS` → `--line-strong`, `--onAccent` → `--on-accent` — so `colorNameMap` is now the identity.
`isReservedMeaning` covers eleven tokens rather than four.

**Phase B — `DESIGN.md` §1–2.** *Patchbay* replaces *Instrument Panel*, the Signal Path is the
signature, and the breaker is recorded as the outgoing one that M16 retires. `### Grounds and
lines` carries 24 rows, `### Label tiers` 4, `### Colour` 12, and a new `### Increased contrast`
overlay 9. Every table gained a `Role` column and a `Contrast (dark)` column. Line 8's reference
implementation becomes the console mock, and the prototype's `?pane=&tab=` deep-link clause is
dropped rather than carried over: the console mock has no query-string routing, so the sentence
would have described a file that does not do it.

**Phase C — the parser and the parity suite.** `ColorRow` gains `role` and `documentedDarkContrast`;
`contrastRows(in:)` reads the overlay. `colorsDocumentToCode` compares four contexts and the role;
`contrastOverlayNameSetsMatchExactly` is new. `LightAppearanceTests` gained a dark ratio column, a
per-appearance contrast-authoring check, and a named six-token exemption list.

**Phase D — `ContrastFloorTests`.** Role-implied pairs at three rungs, not a cross-product. The
ungated roles print their WCAG clause and their measured ratio.

**Phase E — the register.** Four-way read on `Appearance`, all four Swift values recorded per row,
both ratchets extended, `theMetricNameMapCoversTheWholeLadder` added with a named exemption list
that re-reads the mock. Regenerated: **89 rows, 70 matched, 19 pending, 0 uncited** — better than
the plan's expected 64+ matched.

**Phase F — the documents.** `ORCHESTRATOR.md`:18, `PRD.md` §9.1 / §9.9 / §9.10 / §9.11,
`SWIFT_PRACTICES.md` §5. `planning/test-campaign/` untouched, as the plan requires.

## Deviations from the plan, and why

1. **`darkOnAccentDeviationIsPinned` is ported, not replaced (against D5).** Both out-of-family
   lanes reached this independently and the plan's own review record weights it first. `--accent`
   stays the fill under `--on-accent` on every surface until M16–M22 migrate them, so deleting the
   pin removes the only automated measurement of the live pairing during the window it still ships
   in. The plan's compensating assertion — that `--accent` is not the fill under `--on-accent`
   anywhere in `ColorToken` — is dropped as unenforceable, exactly as the review says: the type
   declares values and carries no fill/label pairing to read. The new figures are asserted in a
   test of their own, `accentInkClearsTheFloorUnderItsLabel`.
2. **The light shortfall is new and is recorded.** `--on-accent` on `--accent` is 3.52:1 in light
   as well as 3.23:1 in dark, because this direction takes Apple's published blue rather than the
   darkened `#0069CF` the previous palette used. The pin asserts both.
3. **`ContrastRole` is declared on `ColorToken` and carried as a `Role` column in §2**, rather than
   living in the test file. This is a widening against the plan and it is the shape both review
   lanes proposed independently. It is what makes `--accent-ink`'s inverted suffix answerable by a
   check rather than by a comment: the name says ink, the role says `fill`, and the role is the one
   with a measurement behind it.
4. **A third rung at 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11) was added**, which the plan's two-floor table left out —
   fable's finding. `--accent` and `--focus` take it; the tightest live pair is `--accent` on light
   `--chrome` at 3.12:1, so it is a ratchet rather than a formality.
5. **The three indicator hues get a named ungated role rather than a floor.** `--live` measures
   2.22:1 and `--attn` 2.31:1 on the light ground. Gating them at 3:1 would be red on the design of
   record's own values — the uninterpretable red gate the plan's sequencing section exists to
   avoid. They carry `pairedWithAWord`, whose claim is WCAG 1.4.11's own carve-out for a graphic
   that is never required to understand the content, and a test asserts `DESIGN.md` still states
   the rule that makes the claim true. The ratios are printed rather than skipped.
6. **The five light-only tokens take their `:root` value in dark, as a recorded decision.**
   `--tl-close`, `--tl-min`, `--tl-zoom`, `--accent-wash` and `--accent-wash-line` have only
   `mock.light` in the register, so it is not an oracle for their dark values. It was not invented:
   the mock declares them once in `:root` and re-declares them in neither the dark media block nor
   `.is-dark`, so the cascade resolves them to that value in every context. The traffic lights do
   not change with appearance on the platform either, and a wash is defined by what it modifies.
   `LightAppearanceTests` exempts exactly these five plus `--on-accent`, by name and with the
   reason, rather than relaxing the rule.
7. **The plan's census of 45 `M21-*` rows is 50.** The review verified this against the repo and
   the plan left it undispositioned. The five composite shadow rows — `--shadow-card`,
   `--shadow-pop`, `--shadow-sheet`, `--shadow-tile`, `--shadow-window` — carry
   `M21-owns-the-mock-token-block` and are **declared out of scope here** rather than modelled: a
   shadow is an offset, a blur and a colour, which `ColorToken` cannot hold, and a `ShadowToken`
   would be the new axis the plan sized this item as *not* having. They keep their citation and
   stay `pending`, which is why the census is 19 pending rather than 14.
8. **`ColorRow` does not gain four contrast value fields.** The overlay is returned as its own rows
   and composed by the parity test. A parser that merged the two tables would make a value's
   provenance unrecoverable, and which table a value came from is the thing the overlay exists to
   assert.
9. **`DesignDocParser.contrastRows` sits in an extension** at the bottom of its own file, because
   adding it to the enum body took it to 253 lines against SwiftLint's 250.
10. **Two files were split** for the same ceiling: `MockTokenMetricMapTests.swift` out of
    `MockTokenParityTests.swift` at 433 lines, following the seam `MockTokenLiterals.swift` already
    established.
11. **`Popover radius`'s cell lost `card radius 10–14`.** With `Card radius` a row of its own, the
    prose was the same value in a second place — the defect that table's own note is about.
12. **Three affected-file fixes** the sweep found beyond the plan's known set: the
    `ColorToken.accent` prefix match in `ShellAppearanceTests`, the token extraction in
    `scripts/acceptance/shells.sh` pointing at a file the values left, and the contrast axis in
    `DesignSystemTests`. Each is in its own commit with its reasoning.

## What is open

- **The residual exposure is unchanged and is the point of the item's scope.** Every surface now
  has a text-safe token and no surface has been made to take one. A board drawing a 13pt label in
  `--accent` on `--ground` still ships 3.52:1. `DESIGN.md` §10 records it as the largest thing the
  document asks for and does not enforce; M16–M22 own it per surface.
- **The two increased-contrast contexts have never been rendered.** No engine here applies
  `prefers-contrast`, so they are a specification with computed ratios behind them and no
  photograph. Stated in §2 rather than waived.
- **The cursor prohibition** in the brief (`spec-M21.md`:72-74) has no AC and is not implemented
  here. It is already covered where it lands: `DESIGN.md` §3 rule 8 states it, and
  `ShellChrome`'s A10 declaration plus `ShellAppearanceTests` assert the shell sets no cursor. This
  item adds nothing to it and adds no new surface that could violate it.
- **`SettingsMetrics.cardRadius` derives 10 from `selectionRadius + inset/2`** and now duplicates
  the new `Card radius` token. Repointing it is a one-line change in a board file, which this item
  does not touch — four other items are rewriting those files. Recorded, not fixed.
- **`mac-shell.sh`'s scroll-edge step is marginal**, clearing its 0.90 uniformity bar at 0.872.
  Worth an item of its own if it fails again under load; it is not this item's to fix.
