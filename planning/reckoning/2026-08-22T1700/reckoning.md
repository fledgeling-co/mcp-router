# Adjudication — 2026-08-22, 17:00 (second run)

## The ratchet is clean, and that is the load-bearing result

`reckon.py ratchet` against the 15:26 ledger: **clean, exit 0.** No item left `unmeasured` by any
route other than being measured. That is the slow failure this skill exists to catch across runs —
an item quietly reclassified until nothing remembers it was never checked — and it did not happen.

It is also the only axis on which the two runs differ meaningfully, which is the rest of this note.

## Between the two runs: 37 commits, two merges, and zero rows changed class

`R17` merged at `54666f7` after five verifications. `M15` merged at `29d5111`. `R19` was verified
Done. Merged-tree gates were re-run on both: `make test` went 1697 → 1699 → 1725, lint 530 → 536 →
549 files.

**The partition moved by three rows, and all three are briefs written in the interval** —
`R19-integration`, `G5-gapfix-2`, and `R20` arriving on main with R17's merge. Nothing was
reclassified. Nothing retired. `verified-done` sits at 112 in both.

So the honest reading of this run is not about the backlog. It is about the instrument:

> **This reckoning cannot see a day's work.** Two items merged and the `unbuilt` count went
> **up**, 75 → 78.

**Two — the campaign registry is frozen.** `planning/test-campaign/` carries 15 tracked

`unmeasured`, `undecided` and `waived` are all necessarily identical. Five of the seven classes are
pinned by a directory this reckoning may only read.

## What that means for the numbers above

The headline — *167 pieces of work remain, 152 product* — is **wrong in a knowable direction and by
a knowable amount**: subtract the 62 merged-or-done and the product figure is nearer **90**, of
which 13 defects are genuinely open by the inventory's own status field and about 16 briefs are
genuinely unstarted.

The `requirements_observed` axis is the one untouched by all of this, because requirements join to
cases directly and never pass through the brief join. It reads **11 of 25**, unchanged, and it is
still the honest headline: **the code is in better shape than its evidence.**

## What to do first, and the order changed since this morning

**One — fix the join, and it is now the highest-value thing in this document.** This morning it was
a recommendation; two runs in, it is demonstrated: the instrument produced an identical answer
across a day that merged two items and closed three verifications. A report that cannot move is a
report that stops being read, and the fix is a convention — a brief names the requirement or case
it answers to — not a tool.

**Two — the twelve `unmeasured` requirements are unchanged and remain evidence-work**, owned by
whoever owns the harness, each needing its own remedy. `REQ-007` still goes first: *numbers the
router does not observe are never displayed* is the guarantee the product rests on, and nothing has
watched it hold.

**Three — the campaign registry needs to land.** Not this session's to commit, and it is the reason
five of seven classes cannot move. Until it does, re-running this skill measures the brief queue
and nothing else.

Everything the 15:26 run could not, unchanged, plus one thing it could not have known: **a second
run at the same inputs tells you about the instrument rather than the project.** The three-row
delta here is the reckoning observing the briefs I wrote between the two runs, which is the closest
thing to a self-measurement this pipeline has, and it is not a coverage figure.
