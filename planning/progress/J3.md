# J3 — pixel claims standing on JSON

The raster-visual rung is the campaign's one claim that somebody looked at a screen. Three findings
from one `campaign.py check` run said it was not carrying that meaning, and they overlapped by case
id, which is the tell that they were one cause rather than three: **the rung accepted a claim
without demanding a distinct image, from a named channel, of the thing being claimed.**

## What was there, measured

`campaign.py check planning/test-campaign` at `test-campaign` 0.14.1, full scope, 2026-08-29, over
114 cases with 32 on the raster rung:

| Finding | Count | Cases |
|---|---|---|
| pixel claims citing an artifact that is not a picture | 28 | CASE-0161, 0163, 0165, 0171, 0173, 0175, 0185, 0186, 0187, 0191, 0192 |
| cases publishing one PNG byte for byte | 3 | CASE-0185, 0186, 0187 |
| pixel claims naming no capture method | 5 | CASE-0185, 0186, 0187, 0191, 0192 |

Eleven distinct cases across three findings. Nothing was fabricated — every one of the eleven also
cited a real PNG — but a rung that accepts a manifest as its artifact cannot tell those eleven from
a case citing only JSON, and one of those was already in the registry: `CASE-0109` cites
`evidence/runs/mac-glass-capture.log` and nothing else, held honest by an `n/a:` status rather than
by any check.

## What shipped

### 1. `planning/test-campaign/bin/raster-claim-gate.py`, in `make lint`

Six rules over `cases.json`, ten control arms on every invocation.

| Rule | Refuses |
|---|---|
| R1 | a pixel claim naming no artifact |
| R2 | an artifact that is not a raster by its own first bytes, is zero-byte, or is 1x1 |
| R3 | two pixel claims on one file's bytes, unless every case in the group declares `capture.sharesWith` naming the others and a `capture.sharesReason` |
| R4 | no `capture.channel`, a channel outside the closed vocabulary, or no `capture.method` |
| R5 | a `capture.derivedFrom` that is not itself a picture the repository holds |
| R6 | any cited path the INDEX does not hold |

**Why a repository gate when the skill already checks two of the three.** `campaign.py check` found
all of this and it cannot be the enforcement. `vendor/test-campaign/` pins **0.9.2**, whose
`campaign.py` has neither `RASTER_RUNGS` nor `inspect_raster`; the version that has them is
whichever one a shared plugin cache happens to hold, which `vendor/README.md` says outright is
"shared, and it moves". A rule enforced only there leaves with the cache.

**The closed channel vocabulary** is a reading of the evidence rather than a label applied to it:
every member was counted off the `capture.method` strings the cases already carried.

| Channel | Cases | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| `display-server-window` | 16 | window-scoped off the OS display server |
| `simulator-render-server` | 6 | off a booted simulator the harness owns |
| `browser-raster` | 1 | a browser engine's rasteriser over a live URL |
| `offscreen-render` | 2 | the hosting view's own backing store, no window ordered in |

`offscreen-render` is the one that had to be added rather than derived, and it is the whole point
of R4: SURF-028's `shotNote` already said these frames are "rendered off an NSHostingView's own
backing store by MeasureDump (`cacheDisplay(in:to:)`), not photographed off the display", and no
case carried that distinction. A reader could not tell a photograph of a running window from a
render, which is exactly what the third finding is.

**What the gate does not do**, stated rather than implied. It does not score a picture — no
density, entropy or unique-colour floor separates a failed capture from a legitimately sparse empty
state. It does not adjudicate whether a picture is OF its subject; that is `capture-manifest.py`,
`capture-lineage.py` and `G17-L1`'s axis. It reaches `cases.json` only, not the published wall. And
a case that did not resolve to `pass` is out of scope — four are, `CASE-0101`, `CASE-0109`,
`CASE-0110` and `CASE-0111`, and holding a case that recorded it never reached a capture to
producing one would push it toward a picture manufactured to clear a gate.

### 2. The eleven cases, adjudicated one at a time

Every disposition was decided by reading which artifact each case's own check opens.
`bin/g17_document_assert.py` is the ground truth for the swiftui five: `art.dump`, `art.text_of`,
`art.blob` and `art.node` read the view dump, `art.wire` reads the router's response, and
`art.figure_fraction` is the only one that opens a PNG.

**Six mac-glass cases — the picture was right, the citation list was not.** CASE-0161, 0163, 0165,
0171, 0173 and 0175 each cited a real, distinct, window-scoped PNG *plus* `captures.json` and, for
two of them, `mac-glass-capture.log`. Both of those are real provenance and neither is a capture,
so they moved to `capture.source`, which `evidence-citation-gate.py`'s `EVIDENCE_KEYS` already
reaches — the citation is kept and the claim that it is a photograph is dropped.

**CASE-0185 to `structural-visual`.** Its checks are G17-P1, G17-P2 and G17-P3, and all three read
`readme.served.dump.json`: a titlebar's text, a heading and a sentence, three tab labels, and the
absence of `trawl` from the tree. Not one opens the PNG. A true structural claim wearing the
strongest rung's badge on a frame it never read.

**CASE-0186 and CASE-0187 to the crop each one measures.** These two do read pixels — G17-P4 counts
the band's colour over the figure card's frame, G17-P5 requires under 1% of it over the
`figure-refusal` node's frame — so the rung was right and the picture was not. Both cited the whole
1536x1640 frame, which does not show which card was measured and which CASE-0185 cited too.
`figure_fraction` now writes the region it counted:

| Case | Artifact | Size | sha256 |
|---|---|---|---|
| CASE-0186 | `readme.served.figure-card.png` | 1472x262 | `89d55cf5…` |
| CASE-0187 | `readme.served.figure-refusal.png` | 708x28 | `83d6831e…` |

Both carry `capture.derivedFrom` naming the parent frame, and verdicts.json records each crop's
box, its sha256 and the parent's. Written only when `expect_fail is None` — a crop taken during an
arming run would be a picture of the planted fault filed under the good frame's name.

**Re-shooting was considered and is not available.** One render draws both the resolved figure and
the escaping reference's placeholder, so two runs of the harness produce two identical frames; the
notes on both cases record that the arming controls restore and re-render *byte-identically*. The
thing that distinguishes the two claims is the region, not the run.

**CASE-0191 and CASE-0192 to `outcome`.** G17-W4 reads `reason == "noPackageDirectory"` off the
router's own response and G17-W5 reads `reason`, `file` and `limit` off it; G17-R1 and G17-R2 read
the state titles out of the two refusal dumps. Neither case opens its PNG. `outcome` rather than
`structural-visual` because these two cases are the **only** home of G17-W4 and G17-W5 — CASE-0184
owns W1 to W3 and nothing else owns W4 and W5 — so demoting them to the drawn half alone would
delete the only outcome-rung reading of two live wire refusals. Their dump files were added to
`evidence`, which they had never cited, and their PNGs stay cited as the frames those dumps came
from, claimed now as evidence of nothing.

**Why that red is not this branch's.** Stated as a measurement rather than as a claim about
intent: `planning/sweep-controls.json` on this branch differs from `main` by exactly one row
added — `planning/test-campaign/bin/raster-claim-gate.py` — with none removed and none changed,
the two failing rows byte-identical to `main`'s, and `git diff main...HEAD -- scripts/` empty. The
branch cannot have slowed a script it does not touch. What is NOT established is whether those two
controls are slow or merely starved: the machine carried a load average near 900 from concurrent
fleet runners throughout, and a 120-second timeout is the kind of thing contention alone can trip.
Both readings were taken under that load, so the comparison holds and the absolute number does not.

## Two corrections made on the second pass

**Four `sourceNote` strings claimed a run log the case does not cite.** The note moved onto all six
mac-glass cases read "Both are real provenance … and the run log carries the attach and the select".
Only CASE-0161 and CASE-0171 cite `mac-glass-capture.log`; CASE-0163, CASE-0165, CASE-0173 and
CASE-0175 cite `captures.json` alone. A false sentence in an evidence record is the same defect
class this item is about — a record asserting more than it holds — so the four now carry a
single-source note naming only `captures.json`, and the two keep the pair.

**A stray blank line** left between `_write_crop` and `class Blocked` in
`bin/g17_document_assert.py`, removed. The assert re-runs 18 green and rewrites both crops
byte-identically, so the tree does not move when the check is run.

## What was not done, and why

**The rung's own gloss says "pixels were captured off a display server", and two honest cases now
declare `offscreen-render`.** That is a real tension and it is upstream's: `ORACLE_RUNGS` lives in
the skill, there is no rung between `structural-visual` and `raster-visual` for a measured
offscreen render, and demoting CASE-0186 and CASE-0187 would delete the only checks in the campaign
that can say an image *drew* rather than that a view was laid out. Recorded here and left declared
rather than resolved by moving a case.

**`CASE-0109` was left exactly as it is** — `raster-visual`, `n/a:` with a reason saying the status
item is not pressable in the background, citing only a log. It is the shape the gate exists to
refuse and it is honest about it, which is why the gate's population is the resolved-pass cases.
