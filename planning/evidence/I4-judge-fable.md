The safeguards that make it responsible: the trust grant must originate on the *Mac* (the Mac authorizes a named phone; the phone cannot authorize itself), installs restricted to the registry with pinned versions rather than arbitrary URLs, recent biometric on the phone per install, a Mac notification on every install, a visible "this device can install" indicator, and one-tap revoke.
