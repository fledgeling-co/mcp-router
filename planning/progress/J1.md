# J1 — the channel 145 briefs never wrote to

The brief's finding, restated as the thing that was built: every join edge between this
repository's brief queue and its campaign registry is a **citation somebody typed**, and most
briefs type none. A brief that names nothing cannot be retired however much ships, so the
remaining-work total cannot fall, and `unjoined` — "nobody has looked at this" — is
indistinguishable from "this shipped and nothing recorded it".

Built: `planning/brief-target-gate.py`, a `make lint` member that partitions every brief by how it
names its target, blocks a new brief that names nothing, and refuses a declared id no registry
holds. Its floor is `planning/brief-target-ratchet.json`.

**No existing brief was edited.** Not one citation was written into the queue. The measurements
below that involve moving the join were taken on scratch copies of `planning/features-to-triage`,
and the section that follows them says why doing it for real is the failure the item exists to
prevent.

## The two numbers this rests on, re-measured on this branch

`reckon 1.7.0` over `planning/features-to-triage` and `planning/test-campaign`, at `5ceef5d`:

    join 42/187 (22.5%)  weak=True   work=154   unjoined=82   decision-work=86

The brief carried 29/167 (17.4%) from the 2026-08-29 reckoning on `main`. This branch's queue is
twenty briefs larger — the wave filed them — and the rate is the same story at a different
denominator. `weak=True` is the operative fact: below 50% `reckon` withholds retirement across the
board, so **at this rate no brief can leave the remaining set however green it is.**

## The channel, and why it is an existing one

`reckon.read_briefs` takes frontmatter `reckon-sources` / `sources` / `source` as `source_ids`, and
`reckon.build_join` unions those into its cited set at **confidence 1.0**. Frontmatter is stripped
before the prose id scan, so the two channels cannot double-count.

Inventing a new field would have produced one this gate is happy about and `reckon` cannot see, so
the control's last arm measures the claim instead of asserting it: it writes a two-brief fixture
queue, runs the real `reckon 1.7.0` against the real campaign registry, and fails unless the
declared brief carries a `cited` edge and the bare one lands `unjoined`.

**That arm earned its place on its first run.** It reported `declared -> NO EDGE`, and the defect
was in the reader, not the claim: the anchor `"edges": [`, `planning/reckoning/2026-08-29/ledger.json:71`
at `5ceef5d` sits under `join`, and the gate was looking for it at the ledger's top level. Every
agreement would have been silently unmeasured, which is the exact shape of the thing being gated.

`egress`'s `DEF-253`.**

## The `unbuilt` class is another project's defect id, twice

`reckon` reads a cited id the registry does not hold as evidence the thing is *absent from the
product* — `unbuilt`, product work. That reading is right for a citation somebody meant, and it is
why a wrong id manufactures a finding rather than a warning.

`planning/target-resolution-gate.py` already knows better: it declares `DEF-252`, `DEF-253` and
`REQ-999` foreign, with the evidence, and passes them at severity 0. `reckon` has no such
vocabulary. So `G9`'s brief, which cites `egress`'s `DEF-253` as prior art, and this item's own
brief, which quotes `G9`'s finding, are the two rows behind the reckoning's `unbuilt` line —
the anchor `named in a brief; nothing in the registry answers to it`,
`planning/reckoning/2026-08-29/reckoning.md:23` at `5ceef5d`. **Not one row of that product work is
about this product.**

`FOREIGN_ONLY` names it, counts it and prints the disagreeing instrument. It does not block, and
that is a decision rather than an omission: the only edits `reckon` respects would be fencing the
token — mangling a record's prose to satisfy a tool — or attaching a target the brief may not have.
Both are worse than the disagreement being visible.

## Where the total actually moves, which is not where it looks

`planning/evidence/J1/reckon-lever.txt`, both arms on scratch copies.

Ten `unjoined` briefs given a declared target: **join 42→52, `unjoined` 82→72, and remaining work
`+0`.** They pointed at `DEF-043`, an open defect, so all ten moved from `unjoined` to `broken` —
out of "nobody looked" and into what the registry actually says. That is the join working, and it
is a reclassification rather than a reduction.

Every `unjoined` brief given a target that is a surface passing at or above the `outcome`
retirement rung:

    BEFORE  join  42/187 (22.5%) weak=True   work=154  decision-work=86
    AFTER   join 124/187 (66.3%) weak=False  work=154  decision-work=3, bookkeeping=83

**The weak-join floor lifts and 83 briefs become `retirable`.** The headline scalar does not move,
because `reckon` counts `bookkeeping` inside `work_items`: the 83 stop being "somebody has to read
this and rule" and become "this is done, go and retire it". Retiring them is
`planning/shipped-brief-gate.py`'s half — G31, the stopgap that named this join rate as the real
lever and could not reach it.

So of the brief's five acceptance clauses, three are met, one is now reachable and measured, and
one is owed elsewhere. Which is which is in the section below.

## What was deliberately not done

**No brief was retrofitted.** The dispatch forbids mass-editing the 75 (82 here) to invent
citations, and `reckon`'s own guidance and `shipped-brief-gate`'s docstring both record the same
failure: a citation written to satisfy a tool moved one project's join from 6.2% to 100% while both
gates still exited 0. The gate holds the backlog at a floor that may only fall and blocks what is
new, which is the mechanism the brief asked for; the 82 stay counted, visibly, until somebody reads
one and rules on it.

So **acceptance clause 4 — "the join rate rises above the half floor" — is not met on this branch,
by choice.** It is met on the scratch measurement above, and what that measurement shows is that
the clause is reachable: 66.3% and `weak=False` the moment the existing set carries real targets.
Clause 5 — "shipping something moves the total" — needs the retirement step as well as the join.

**`ABSENT_ONLY` is reported and not blocked.** `target-resolution-gate.py`'s `ID_ABSENT` arm
already blocks a prose id no registry holds. Two gates blocking one token is how two vocabularies
come to disagree about one word.

**The corpus is the git index, not the working tree.** A staged brief is caught before it lands; an
unstaged draft is not. That follows `evidence-citation-gate` (`G24`) and `target-resolution-gate`,
and it avoids the harm `reader-accounting.py` measured when its first cut walked the working
directory and reddened a shared branch over another session's untracked work. The cost is real and
it is printed rather than argued away: briefs on disk and not in the index are counted and named in
every run.

**`reckon` is not vendored.** It lives in the machine's plugin cache, so on a checkout without it
the agreement arm reports `NOT RUN` by name and the other eighteen still fire. A green control
there is eighteen arms, not nineteen, and `planning/sweep-controls.json` records that under
`not_proved`.
