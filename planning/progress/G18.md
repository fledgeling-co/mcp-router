# G18 — the campaign's surface list is hand-maintained, and went stale twice over

Closes the mechanism, not the instances. G15, G16 and G17 add the three missing surfaces; this adds
the thing that would have named them the day they shipped.

## What was wrong with the gate that existed

`campaign.py check` asks **which enumerated surface has no case**. Every enumerated surface has one,
so it reported clean — and it reported clean across two milestones while the app shipped three
surfaces the campaign had never heard of. M22 added the Harnesses and Insights boards; M30 added the
capability-document viewer. The campaign's surface list is written by hand and none of the three
followed it.

A surface that was never enumerated is invisible to a check keyed on enumeration. M32 closed the
same shape for mock elements by making the census total: an element outside every rule could not be
reported by a gate that reports by rule. The denominator has to come from somewhere other than the
list being checked.

## Where the denominator comes from now

`planning/test-campaign/bin/surface-oracle.swift` is **compiled against the shipped
`Destination.swift` and `RouterSheet.swift`** and prints what the app itself says it ships:
9 destinations and 16 sheet kinds, of which 13 are hosted and 3 are drawn in the mock with no board
able to present them.

Compiled rather than parsed, and the reason is measured rather than stylistic — M35 hit the first
half of it today in `scripts/acceptance/mac-shell.sh`:

- `Destination.title` is a `switch` that does not follow the case names. `.evals` reads `Checks`.
  A `case`-line scraper prints `Evals`, and the campaign's surface is called *Mac Checks board*.
- `RouterSheet.Kind.isHosted` is computed from `owner`, another `switch`. All sixteen kinds look
  identical as `case` lines. A parser cannot tell a shipped sheet from one M22 still owes, so it
  would demand campaign coverage of three sheets that do not exist.

`Destination.ordered` is `allCases` and `Kind.allCases` is `CaseIterable`, both generated by the
compiler from the declarations. A destination cannot be added to this app without appearing in the
count.

## What is derived and what is written by hand

Address is derived; expectation is written. The oracle says **where a surface lives and what it is
called**; `planning/test-campaign/surface-bindings.json` says **which campaign surface covers it**.

That split is M35's and it is load-bearing. A fully derived binding would invent a campaign surface
for every address the app grows, so the denominator would move with the app and stop catching the
one thing this exists to catch: a surface shipped with nobody having decided how to test it. A human
still writes the row. What a human no longer has to do is notice that the row is missing.

## The classes are separate because the remedies are

Conflating "shipped and unenumerated" with "enumerated and uncovered" is how this stayed invisible,
so `surface-reconcile.py` never merges them:

| class | what it means | remedy |
|---|---|---|
| SHIPPED-UNENUMERATED | the app presents it; the campaign has no surface | enumerate a surface and write cases — nobody has decided how to test this yet |
| ENUMERATED-UNCOVERED | a surface exists and no case cites it | write a case — somebody decided how to test it and did not |
| BOUND-TO-UNKNOWN | a binding names a surface `inventory.json` does not hold | fix the binding, or restore the surface |
| STALE-BINDING | bound to an address the app no longer ships | retire the binding, and check the surface is not testing something gone |
| BOUND-BUT-UNSHIPPED | bound to a sheet kind no board can present | retire the binding — a surface for an unpresentable sheet is a paper surface |
| UNEXPLAINED-ADDRESSLESS | a Mac campaign surface nothing binds and nothing explains | bind it, or record why it has no product address |
| SEED-ORPHAN | a `*.in.json` row the live set no longer holds | restore the row, or record its retirement |

The last two close the reverse direction. Without UNEXPLAINED-ADDRESSLESS the campaign could grow
Mac surfaces the product does not have and nothing would say so; `SURF-001` (the shell frame) and
`SURF-009` (the menu-bar popover) are the two that legitimately have no product address today, and
each carries its reason in the bindings file rather than being silently skipped.

## The requirement list's two sources

The brief reads `requirements.in.json` at 20 and `inventory.json` at 25 as five rows added to one
side with nothing reconciling them. Measured across all six pairs, that is the general shape rather
than a requirement-specific slip, and it is not drift:

```
requirements.in.json  20 -> 25      cases.in.json      47 -> 76
surfaces.in.json      17 -> 24      defects.in.json    18 -> 57
components.in.json     8 ->  8      flows.in.json       5 ->  5
```

Nothing in the vendored campaign machinery reads a `*.in.json` file at all — `campaign.py` ingests
one once through `add --file`, and everything after that is `inventory.json` and `cases.json`. The
seeds are the campaign's opening enumeration and the live set is meant to grow past them: 47 cases
becoming 76 is the campaign working. **Demanding equality would force an input file to be rewritten
on every added case, which is a second hand-maintained list and the exact illness this item is
about** — so equality is recorded as the wrong invariant rather than enforced.

The invariant that is meaningful runs the other way: nothing the campaign opened with may vanish
from the live set. That is silent narrowing, and it is what the requirement list would actually be
guilty of if a requirement it started with stopped being tracked. All six pairs satisfy it today,
and `SEED-ORPHAN` fires if one stops. It was armed by planting `REQ-999` into the seed.

## First run

Exit 1, naming three, which are exactly the three the brief predicted:

```
    FAIL destination:harnesses  (Harnesses)  [Running]
    FAIL destination:insights   (Insights)   [Library]
    FAIL sheet:readme           (readme)     [in-mock]
```

`sheet:readme` is the capability-document viewer — `RouterSheet.Servers.document(server:)`, served
by M30's `GET /servers/:name/document`. None of G15, G16 or G17 had landed when this ran, so it
named all three. Each of those items turns one row green by adding a binding and a case; writing the
bindings here would have turned the gate green while the campaign still had no surface and no case
for any of them, which is the defect with a fresh coat on it.

The gate is therefore in `make all` and red on landing, on purpose.

## Armed

Every red exit was watched to fire and every mutated file restored byte-identically, by hash.

| control | planted | verdict | restore |
|---|---|---|---|
| SHIPPED-UNENUMERATED, through the real compile | a tenth `Destination` case with no campaign surface | exit 1, delta was exactly `destination:probeboard`, nothing else moved | `Destination.swift` sha256 `c888470…` before and after |
| BLOCKED (exit 3) | a syntax error in `surface-oracle.swift` | exit 3, `BLOCKED — the surface oracle did not build`, no verdict | `surface-oracle.swift` sha256 `93f4934…` before and after |
| CONTROL FAILED (exit 2) | the `shipped_unenumerated` append replaced with `pass` | exit 2, no verdict printed | `surface-reconcile.py` sha256 `f7b56f8…` before and after |
| SEED-ORPHAN | `REQ-999` in `requirements.in.json`, absent from inventory | exit 1, `FAIL REQ-999` | `requirements.in.json` sha256 `9abbe51…` before and after |

`make surface-reconcile-arm` is the first of those as a repeatable target; it writes
`evidence/runs/arm-surface-reconcile.json`.

The other six classes are covered by a **presence control that runs on every invocation**: each is
planted into an in-memory copy of the world and required to be reported, plus a negative arm that
requires an explained addressless surface to stay quiet. If any arm fails the gate exits 2 and
prints no verdict at all, because a count from an instrument that cannot see the defect is not
evidence. That control was itself watched to fire, in the exit-2 row above.

The distinction between the presence control and `arm-surface-reconcile.py` is the one
`arm-pipeline.py` already draws for the glass suite: the control proves the **classifier** bites,
the arm proves the **chain that feeds it** bites — a real `swiftc` run against the real file.

## Not fixed here, seen while working

`planning/test-campaign/ledger.md`'s header reads *20 surfaces · 70 cases* against an inventory
holding 24 and 76. It is generated by `campaign.py report` and has not been regenerated since those
grew — the same staleness family, on a file this item does not own. Regenerating it is a large
unrelated diff and belongs with whoever next runs the campaign report.
