## Four calls I made, and what each cost

**`isApprovable` has three conditions, not the two step 12 names.** The band has no requirement
fields, and `RegistryCapability.declaration(for:values:)` does not refuse an entry with unmet
requirements — it drops the empty values and sends the rest. So an entry asking for an API key would
have reached the router as a blank credential, which is the failure `RegistryCapability.action`'s own
comment records having already found once as dead code. It bites on a recorded fixture:
`smithery:deepwiki` asks for an `Authorization` header, and its rendered row carries no `Approve`.
**Cost:** a row that could be installed from the band in the plan's reading cannot be in this one. It
keeps `Review…`, which is where the fields are.

**`Not now` is a second string and the board keeps `Decline`.** Step 13 offered the fork on that
condition; the board was checked and it holds. The mock spells this state three ways across three
surfaces, so the divergence is the design of record's rather than this item's.

**`(prominent)` on `Approve` was not taken, and this is the one place the mock loses.** `DESIGN.md:212`
binds accent exposure to a live accessibility deviation that
`LightAppearanceTests.darkOnAccentDeviationIsPinned` measures every run, and bounds it to one filled
action per view. The band caps at three rows and the footer already holds this view's slot, so
prominence would have put up to four accent fills in the smallest surface in the app. The same passage
names the substitute — *"distinguished by shape and position too"* — so `Approve` takes the mock's
leading position with the standard fill. **Cost:** the mock's emphasis is weaker on this control. It is
declared as `D2` in `popover.pairing.tsv` where the conversion gate reads it, rather than absorbed.

**`⌥⌘Q` stays refused and `DESIGN.md` §8 gained a second limb rather than an exception.** The rule
tested the command and the gap was in the key. Referred to two lanes — `agy` /
`gemini-3.7-flash-high` and `claude-fable-5` at high effort, both answering Option A independently,
both naming a mechanism neither the brief nor the verdict had, and I took it after measuring the
binding on this machine myself. `codex` is usage-limited to 2026-08-27 and `grok-4.6` answers `402`,
both as `spec-M20.md` predicted. I did **not** take gemini's alternative of rebinding the command to
`⌃R` or `⇧⌘R`: inventing a chord the mock never drew is a larger departure from the design of record
than declining one.

## What I could not settle, and what I deliberately did not touch

**The conversion gate cannot run against the popover.** `mock-affordances.py` slices a `.v-<state>`
frame inside a section and `#statusPopover` has none — it is one unframed surface where `#b-servers`
and `#swin` carry state blocks. `mock-fidelity-gate.sh popover` exits **3**, which is the designed
could-not-run exit, and the generated ledger says so in its own words and is committed as that. Two
fixes exist and neither is this item's: a whole-section fallback in a shared gate script whose blast
radius is every surface, or state frames added to the design of record to satisfy an instrument.
Criterion 4 does not depend on it — the structure dump is what the criterion asks for and it is in the
ledger.

**A second blindness in that gate, worth more than the first.** The mock's four header count cells are
plain divs matching none of the derivation rules, so the derived inventory has 14 rows and none of them
is a count. `Resident 214 MB` — the one number in this popover that does not ship — **would never
appear as an absent affordance however long the gate ran.** It is enforced structurally instead, by
`MenuBarPresentation.Counts` having no field it could come from. This is `M32`'s shape, pointed at a
different surface; recorded in `popover.layers.json` rather than filed, because I did not want to open
an item on a judgement about someone else's gate.

**F4 and F5 are not mine.** The gap-fix brief's addendum routes the unmeasurable menu badge to **M34**
and the SwiftPM-cannot-see-`app/MCPRouter` lane to **M33**. Neither was attempted. Both lanes were run
here regardless and both are green.

**One stale figure I made staler and did not fix.** `planning/null-run-gate.py:395` prints *"the Swift
suite (1684 tests)"* in its `NOT_ARMED` narrative. It is printed and never asserted, it was already
stale before M20, and this pass takes the true figure to 1748. The verdict assigned it to nobody and I
am leaving it there, because any number I write into that line goes stale the same way on the next
item — the fix is to derive it or drop it, which is a decision about that gate.

**`make lint` and `make build-mac` as targets did not run**, and are recorded as not run rather than as
passing. Both fail at their `tools` prerequisite in this worktree: no `node_modules`, no
`dist/index.js`. Every component was run individually and is green.

**`mac-shell.sh` end to end did not run.** It needs a Release build as well as Debug. I read the bar
