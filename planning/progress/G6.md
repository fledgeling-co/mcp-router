## The finding

is not what runs in `lint`. Closing that gap is the pre-commit hook `planning/hooks/pre-commit`
(`G9`), which is tracked and deliberately not installed.

## The remedy, decided rather than assumed

The brief asked which of *copy the artifact in* or *refuse the citation* is the rule. **Both, in
that order, and the order is the whole of it.** A load-bearing instrument is committed — a sweep, a
fixture, a mutation harness — and the record cites the repository path. Only where the artifact is
already unrecoverable may the record keep the scratch path, and then only behind a closed
withdrawal marker. The gate implements exactly that: `CITED` blocks, `WITHDRAWN` passes, and there
is no third state where a scratch path is simply tolerated.

The evidence for the ordering is in this item's own history. Nothing was recovered: twelve cited
families, zero survivors, and reconstruction was available and refused because an instrument
rebuilt from the recorded counts is an instrument built to agree with them. *Refuse the citation*
alone would have deleted the distinction between unrecoverable evidence and evidence never
gathered; *copy the artifact in* alone was unavailable a day too late. The marker is what carries
the difference, and it costs one parenthesis.
