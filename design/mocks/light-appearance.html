Light appearance, the nine states, and the gallery

DESIGN.md §10 records light as unauthored. This authors it. Light is not an inversion of dark: every dark indicator hue fails text contrast on a light ground (1.71–2.91:1 against 4.5 needed), so all four are re-solved. Tiers, lines and fills are authored to reproduce dark's measured ratios rather than its alpha values, so hierarchy reads identically in both appearances. The dark reference implementation, prototype.html, is unchanged.

Amber and red are the pair that has to survive, because they mean “wants a decision” and “failed”. Solved on hue alone they land 21.5° apart at near-identical lightness — the region protan and deuteran vision compresses. Pulling amber toward yellow opens that to 39.8°. Colour is still never the only signal: position and the lamp carry the same state.

Housing 30×48 — an 8pt lamp boss on the plate, then the 40pt switch body. The prototype floats its lamp at top:-9px, outside a 40pt housing, which overflows a 56pt row and would be clipped by SwiftUI in most containers; mounting it on the plate removes the defect and is truer to the object. Slot 22×34, toggle 22×15, travel 4→19: the slot is at least as wide as the toggle and strictly taller, so a recess is visible above the toggle when down and below it when up, lit or not. That is the invariant two prototype rounds failed, and it is what makes a dormant row still read as a switch. Springs: 0.18s with overshoot rising, 0.6s without falling. Reduce Motion removes the transition, never the state change.
