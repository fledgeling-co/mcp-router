## 1 — an effect class the census cannot name

`vacuity-check.py`'s two census passes both fail open on an unrecognised class, and in opposite
ways that produce the same silence. `pass_unclassed` skips any requirement with a truthy
`effect`; `pass_uncensused` selects on `effect in EXTERNAL`. REQ-027's `filesystem-read`
satisfied the first and failed the second, so neither pass saw it while `--gate`'s exit code
reported confidently on the other two. `campaign.py check` 0.14.1 does block on it
(`bad_effect_class`, campaign.py:1074), which is why this was visible at all.

**Built:** `planning/test-campaign/bin/effect-class-census.py`, enrolled in `make lint` with its
control. It parses `EFFECT_CLASSES` out of every copy of the instrument this repository can see —
with `ast`, not a regex, because both files mention the tuple in prose as well as defining it —
and reports a disagreement between copies as a finding. Measured: 24 copies across 12 origins, the
`vendor/` pin and eleven cache versions from 0.9.3 to 0.16.0, all carrying the same nine members.

**Decided:** REQ-027 declares `effect: "none"` and records `effectUnrepresentable:
"filesystem-read"`. The vocabulary is not extended — `vendor/README.md` forbids editing the
pinned copy and upstream is a different repository — so the acceptance sketch's first branch is
taken. `none` here means *no representable class*, and the row therefore owes more, not less: the
gate requires the named class to be outside the instrument's set, `effect` to be exactly
`none` so the narrowing is stated rather than implied, and `provider` to RESOLVE through
vacuity-check's own `resolve_provider` against `campaign.json`'s `sourceRoot`. It resolves by
path on `RouterCore/Control/ControlDocument.swift`, so deleting the read site reddens the row even
though it has left the external-effect census.

**The alternative it beat, and the axis it was better on.** `inbound-socket` is defensible — the
route is served on the loopback listener — and it keeps the row inside the instrument's own
external-effect denominator, which is what `none` gives up. It was rejected on a fact neither
consulted reader had: `campaign.py`'s `unbacked_effects` (campaign.py:1078) blocks a requirement
naming an external effect, recorded `observed`, with no passing case at the `effect-witness`
rung. REQ-027's seven cases are structural, structural-visual, raster-visual and metamorphic.
Declaring `inbound-socket` would trade one blocker for another unless a witness were built, and
its subject would be the accept CASE-0183 already witnesses for REQ-026.

Referred to three families with the evidence and the options in no order.
`gpt-5.6-sol` at high effort picked `none` plus a repository-owned check carrying the read
obligation, which is what shipped. `gemini-3.7-flash-high` picked `inbound-socket`, and its
objection to `none` — that the row leaves reachability validation — is exactly what the gate's
provider-resolution requirement answers. The `grok-4.6` lane timed out at 120s and was not
retried; two families were enough to disagree.

**Reach limit.** The gate proves a provider exists in production source. That is the census floor,
not a witness, and no case here observes a read at the boundary.

## 2 — REQ-016 declares an outbound-socket effect and names no provider

There is no symbol to bind it to, measured at `83e5380` rather than assumed:

- `PairingService` has exactly one conformance in production source, `public struct
  FixturePairingService: PairingService`, `app/Sources/MCPRouterKit/Pairing/PairingService.swift:31`.
- The shipped shell wires that fixture by default: `pairing: any PairingService =
  FixturePairingService()`, `app/Sources/MCPRouterUI/Phone/PhoneShell.swift:63`.
- No `URLSession`, `NWConnection`, `NWListener` or `Network.NW` symbol appears anywhere under
  `app/Sources/MCPRouterKit/Pairing/` (9 files). The fourteen files under `app/Sources` that do
  hold one are OAuth, control-plane, registry and loopback-server code, none reachable from the
  pairing path.
- The protocol says so itself: `No live implementation ships in this item, and that is a decision
  rather than a gap.`

So the claim is withdrawn in the sense `references/effect-boundary.md` §2 means: REQ-016 moves
from `contradicted` to `vacuous`. Contradicted means the product does the thing and does it
differently from the document; vacuous means it does not do the thing at all, so there is nothing
for the constraint to be true or false about, and §3 names this exact shape. `campaign.py check`
now reads `vacuous=1 unwitnessed=0` where it read `unwitnessed=1 … REQ-016 (contradicted)`.

**The `provider` field stays empty deliberately**, so `vacuity-check.py --gate` continues to
report REQ-016 as uncensused. That finding is the vacuity, visible — a dispositioned expectation
rather than a silence. The remedy is to build the transport (I5, DEF-001) or withdraw the
requirement; neither is K4's.

## 3 — the blind vocabulary

`campaign.json` declared none, so the pass ran on nine snake_case mutators and seven snake_case
readers over a camelCase Swift corpus. The item's charge was that its recall was unmeasured. It is
measured now, over the 2282 test bodies under `testRoot`: **the defaults find 3 mutating call
sites where a fitted list finds 481.** `blind=3` was a reading of 3 of 481.

**Declared** in `campaign.json` with `only: true` — 19 mutators, 34 readers. The mutator list is
derived mechanically from DEF-055's own causes: every call verb in the corpus counted three ways —
implicit-member expressions, method calls on a receiver, and the verb's own `func` declaration
line — and a verb kept only where method calls are at least half its sites and declarations at most
a quarter. That rule alone removes `restart` (1 method call against 3 declarations, of 5 sites).
A second stage, recorded as a judgement, drops five verbs whose receiver here is a local collection
or scaffolding: `append`, `run`, `apply`, `lock`, `unlock`.

**What the reader list omits, and why that is the load-bearing choice.** `expect` and `require`
are swift-testing's assertion macros, 5781 and 383 occurrences. Declaring either a reader would
mark every test that asserts after mutating as re-reading and collapse the count to near zero
without the pass having learned anything. Four figures over the same bodies, so the vocabulary's
effect is visible rather than buried:

| vocabulary | mutating | re-read | blind |
|---|---|---|---|
| shipped defaults (9/7, snake_case) | 3 | 0 | 3 |
| fitted mutators, no readers at all | 481 | 0 | 481 |
| fitted mutators, 10 tightest readers | 481 | 271 | 210 |
| fitted mutators, the declared 34 readers | 481 | 370 | **111** |

**Armed both directions**, `planning/test-campaign/bin/blind-vocabulary-arm.py`, on `make lint`.
Two plants identical but for one line, each run alone and again inside a `shutil.copytree` of the
real corpus: the blind plant must move examined+1 mutating+1 blind+1, the reading plant examined+1
mutating+1 blind+0. The specificity arm is the half a sensitivity arm alone cannot give — a
detector that flagged every mutating body would pass the first and say nothing. 6 of 6 on cache
0.16.0 and 6 of 6 on the `vendor/` pin. The two differ only in the denominator, 2282 against 2277,
which is the five arrow-style `it/test(` blocks 0.9.3 added detection for, and agree on blind=111.

**The three findings, judged**, each clearing for a different checkable reason:

| finding | fitted mutators in body | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `restartIsIssuedBeforeTheStagingWrite` | 1 — `WatchWorld.remove(scratch)` in a `defer` | NOT blind: `load`, `read`, `parse`, `servers` all follow it |
| `restartIsOwedBeforeTheWriteLands` | 3 — last is `WatchState(restartPending: true).save(` | NOT blind: `load` follows it |
| `restartRechecksTheCamera` | **0** | withdrawn, and UNEXAMINED rather than clean |

The third is the honest one. It was flagged only because `restart` is a shipped default and the
body slice begins at the declaration, so `func restartRechecksTheCamera(` matched itself —
DEF-055's fourth cause. Its real mutation `await model.restart()` is now invisible to the pass,
and its reads are the property comparisons `model.step` and `model.entry.characters` that no
reader verb names. The finding is withdrawn; the test is not thereby proved.

**`vacuity-check.py --gate` goes from findings=4 to findings=112, and that is the work landing,
not a regression.** One is REQ-016's uncensused provider, unchanged and correct. The other 111 are
blind-pass findings the instrument could not previously see, over a corpus whose mutations it was
finding 3 of. They are a worklist: DEF-055 stays OPEN with 0-of-3 measured precision on the
previous population, its structural causes untouched — the pass still examines every `func`
rather than every `@Test`, its helper exclusion is still file-scoped, and a `defer` mutator
still reads as source-early, which is why the first row above needed reading rather than counting.
The campaign publishes no blind number, and `vacuity-check --gate` is not enrolled in `make
lint`; the two gates that are enrolled are the census and the arm.
