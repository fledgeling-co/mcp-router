I3 — iPhone: Triage, Queue and Library

The UI oracle for I3. Every surface, state, interaction and overlay the feature needs, drawn in the design system. Tokens, phone chrome and shared component classes are I1's and I2's, carried over unchanged. The rationale — the rejected interactions, the four prototype corrections, the stated assumptions — is in the stylesheet header, which is part of this document.

The one thing to read first: there is no transport. M6 shipped the Mac's inbox and the wire envelope and deferred the transport itself (D-m6-a); the phone ships a fixture pairing service. So nothing the phone queues can reach the Mac yet, and the phone can observe no Mac-side outcome. Every surface below reports what is locally true and says so.
