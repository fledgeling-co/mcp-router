## What the old instruction actually cost — and what the new pin does not buy

Measured on this registry, four versions of `strict-check.py`:

| Instrument | Reads |
|---|---|
| submodule pin `baaf79ab` → 0.5.0 | **58 of 76 (76%)** |
| 0.9.1 — the version the recorded sitting was run with | **58 of 76 (76%)** |
| vendored 0.9.2 | **62 of 76 (82%)** |
| installed 0.9.4 | **62 of 76 (82%)** |

58 is the number in `strict-ratchet.json`. A clone following the old `git submodule update --init
--recursive` would have printed the recorded figure exactly and read as agreement, while measuring a
definition that has no `effect-witness` rung — 0.5.0's `strict-check.py` contains the string zero
times against 0.9.2's two, and the registry holds exactly four armed `effect-witness` cases. The
four it cannot see are the whole difference. 0.5.0 also has no `capture-lineage.py` and no
`vacuity-check.py`, so two of the four gates could not have run at all.

**The trap is wider than the submodule pin.** 0.9.1 has no `effect-witness` rung either — the
string appears zero times there as well — and it reads 58 on today's registry, `held.` against the
ratchet. So the false confirmation is not 0.5.0's alone: **every version through 0.9.1 prints the
recorded figure and reads as agreement.** The consequence for this item is that **the vendored 0.9.2
does not reproduce the campaign's recorded strict figure.** It reads 62 where `RUN-2026-08-20.md`
records 58, because the recorded reading is a pre-0.9.2 one.

**It is still the right pin, and the mismatch is the reason rather than a cost.** A pin that
reproduced 58 would be a pin to an instrument blind to four of the registry's own armed cases —
reproducing the recorded number by reproducing the blindness that produced it. 0.9.2 is 0.9.1 plus
the DEF-048 fix the campaign itself asked for, it is the version `ORCHESTRATOR.md`, `LEDGER.md`, the
M28 docket and the X7 brief all name, and it reads what the installed 0.9.4 reads on this data. What
should move is the recorded figure: 62 is the correct reading of today's registry under a rung 0.9.1
did not have, and `strict-ratchet.json` is the campaign owner's to raise, not this item's.

## Open, and deliberately untouched

**X7 and X8 are not closed and were not edited.** Their scripts are in the tree now. That changes what a
runner here could do; it does not change what this item did. An edit landing only here forks the
instrument, and a script behaving one way in this repository and another way everywhere else stops
being evidence of anything. Both notes now say that rather than "nothing to edit".

**Which version is "the version the gates ran" is recorded three different ways, and I vendored the
one the repository names.** `ORCHESTRATOR.md`, `LEDGER.md`, the M28 docket and the X7 brief all say
0.9.2, and X7 points at 0.9.2's script directory as the thing to fix. The campaign's own artifacts
disagree: `defects.in.json` stamps three findings `found-by: test-campaign 0.9.1`, and
`RUN-2026-08-20.md`'s fourth sitting is headed 0.9.1. And the machine has moved again —
`installed_plugins.json` records **0.9.4** since 2026-08-21. The three gate scripts that matter are
byte-identical across 0.9.1 and 0.9.2 (`campaign.py`, `capture-lineage.py`, `witness-worklist.py`);
`strict-check.py` differs by 8 lines and `vacuity-check.py` by 80, the latter being 0.9.2's fix for
DEF-048, which the campaign itself reported. So 0.9.2 is 0.9.1 plus the correction the campaign
asked for, and it reproduces 0.9.4's numbers on this data.

**The wrappers are G5-C1, not this item.** Nothing in `Makefile`, `scripts/` or
`planning/test-campaign/bin/` resolves a skill directory, so there was no invocation to repoint. An
agent invoking the skill still loads the installed version.
